# Defend Plane Truth Labels

- `MEASURED`: observed runtime, source, or receipt state.
- `MODELED`: deterministic scoring or rehearsal output.
- `APPROVED`: an independent human approval is present and bound.
- `VERIFIED`: a receipt or runtime contract was independently checked.
- `UNAVAILABLE`: required evidence or capability is absent.

`LIVE` never means authority to act. External containment remains unavailable until a separately reviewed private connector is configured and observed.
